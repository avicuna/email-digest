Email Digest

Email Digest is a Mac app that sorts your Gmail and Proton mail into what needs you, what's worth a glance and what you can skip. It runs on your own Mac so there's no Email Digest server and no account to create.

  • Triage: each refresh sorts new mail with Claude through your own Claude subscription.
  • Drafts: it can write a reply and save it as a draft. It never sends mail.
  • Cleanup: it lists newsletters and marketing senders and only moves mail to Trash when you press a button.
  • News: it turns your newsletters into one short briefing.

Email Digest is in a private alpha for friends and family. Questions: alexv4@pm.me
